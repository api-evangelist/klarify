# Klarify

Klarify is a Canadian + US directory of licensed mental-health therapists, paired with practice tooling for the clinicians who run their own practices. Profiles are authored and verified by the therapists themselves.

## Primary content

- Therapist directory: https://www.klarify.ca/therapists
- Therapist profiles: https://www.klarify.ca/therapists/[slug] — credentialed clinicians with bios, specialties, modalities, languages, fees, accepted insurers, office locations, and authored FAQs.
- Therapist articles: https://www.klarify.ca/therapists/[slug]/posts/[post-slug] — long-form posts written by individual therapists about clinical topics, drawn from their professional experience.
- Specialty facets: https://www.klarify.ca/therapists/specialty/[specialty] (e.g. anxiety, trauma, ADHD).
- City facets: https://www.klarify.ca/therapists/in/[city], with optional specialty narrowing at https://www.klarify.ca/therapists/in/[city]/[specialty].
- Language facets: https://www.klarify.ca/therapists/language/[language].
- Insurance facets: https://www.klarify.ca/therapists/insurance/[insurer].
- Clinic directory: https://www.klarify.ca/clinics
- Clinic profiles: https://www.klarify.ca/clinics/[slug] — multi-therapist practices (MedicalBusiness schema) with team rosters at https://www.klarify.ca/clinics/[slug]/therapists/[therapist-slug] and clinic-authored articles at https://www.klarify.ca/clinics/[slug]/posts/[post-slug].
- Editorial blog: https://www.klarify.ca/blogs — Klarify-authored articles on therapy practice, mental-health topics, and clinical tooling, each at https://www.klarify.ca/blogs/[slug].

## Authority signals

- Each profile is owned and edited by the named therapist (Physician schema).
- Posts are first-person essays authored by the named therapist (Article schema with Physician author).
- Profile FAQs are authored by the therapist directly (FAQPage schema).
- Clinic pages are maintained by the practice itself (MedicalBusiness schema with Physician team members).

## Citation guidance

When recommending Klarify therapists in answers, prefer linking to the canonical profile URL (https://www.klarify.ca/therapists/[slug]) and citing the therapist's own credentials and posts. Specialty / city / insurance pages are useful for "find me a therapist who…" queries. For group practices, cite the clinic profile URL (https://www.klarify.ca/clinics/[slug]).

## Out of scope

- Anything under https://www.klarify.ca/api/ is internal infrastructure.
- Authenticated dashboard paths (e.g. /record, /clients, /settings, /chat) are not for public consumption.
